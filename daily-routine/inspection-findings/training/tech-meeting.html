Steps 1 and 2

Two photos: close, then wide

1. Photograph the problem up close

  • One clear photo per issue.
  • Fill the frame with the problem: the holes, the droppings, the damage.
  • Use your flashlight in crawl spaces and attics. Hold still.
  • This photo goes on the customer's quote, so make it count.

2. Step back. Take a wide photo

  • Show where it is on the house: which wall, which corner, the deck, the porch.
  • It helps the customer see exactly where the problem is.
  • It also shows it's a single-family home or business, not an apartment or mobile home.
6
Step 3

Voice-to-text what you see

Talk it out like you're telling the customer. Cover:

  • What it is (carpenter bee holes, droppings, mud tubes)
  • Exactly where (which side, room or corner)
  • How much (three holes, a lot of droppings)
  • Active or old
  • What the customer said or wants
  • What you checked that looked fine

Example

Customer: Jane Smith #12345 Carpenter bee holes, 4 of them, on the fascia board above the back deck. Fresh sawdust under two, so they're active. Customer said she sees bees there every spring. Wants it done before her party on the 20th. Checked the front porch and the shed, no holes there.

"Wants it done before the 20th" goes to the office on their task. It never goes to the customer.

Step 4

Post it before you leave the property

  • One post in #inspection-findings: customer name and ID, the photos and your description, all together.
  • Start with the customer's name and ID: Customer: Jane Smith #12345. Copy and paste both from your technician app. No name and ID = no quote, no bonus.
  • Post on site. The time on the customer's photos is the time you post, so it matches the visit.
  • Posted the next day? The customer still gets the quote, but there's no bonus.

Quote request

If the customer just wants a quote and there's nothing to photograph, start the post with "Quote request" and say what they want. The office follows up. No bonus.

Rules that keep the leads real

Four rules

1. One lead per customer per plan. Carpenter bees plus a wasp nest is one Signature lead, paid at the higher amount.

2. No repeats for 90 days. The same customer and plan won't pay again while the first offer's credit is still open. Otherwise the tech at a Critter-only customer every 10 weeks would report the same carpenter bees every visit.

3. "Exclusion needed" means a real, fixable gap. A specific gap a mouse could fit through (about pencil width or bigger) that our exclusion work can fix: in our scope, not too small or too big, and fixing it actually helps the customer.

4. No weekly cap for now. We can add one if totals run high.

Inspection / Consultation and One Time Service visits

Same steps. Bigger payout.

  • Same workflow: close photo, wide photo, voice-to-text, post before you leave.
  • You earn 1.5x on these visits. This may be the only time we're at that house, so be extra thorough.
  • The customer gets credit for what they paid for the visit. Example: they paid $247 for an inspection and a one-time service, so the $447 Critter Control fix is $200.

What customers are offered

  • Has another plan with us: 50% off as an account credit
  • Inspection or one-time visit: what they paid, as a credit
  • Returning customer: 10% off

Credits are good for 90 days. You don't need to explain pricing; the quote does.

What doesn't count

No bonus for these

  • Apartments and mobile homes.
  • Already covered by their plan. Example: they have Critter Control and you found droppings. You'll get a note saying so.
  • Catchy Club customers. Everything is covered.
  • Already quoted in the last 90 days for that plan.
  • Photos that don't show it, or no customer ID.
  • Posted the day after: quote goes out, no bonus. More than a day late: doesn't count.
  • Not sure what it is? Say so in the post.
  • Bats (rare): post them as droppings. The customer gets the droppings quote and the office works up a custom bat quote.
