Working a task

Check, call, note, close

1. Check for a YES first. If they already said yes, book it, reply with the date, close the task.

2. Call the customer. No answer: leave a voicemail, note it in the task, close the task.

3. You talked to them: note what happened (closed or not, and why) and close the task.

6
The credit method

It's an account credit, not a discount

  • Say the dollars, not just the percent: "a $173 credit", not "50% off".
  • Call it a current customer account credit. It's theirs, and it expires.
  • Good for 90 days. The task and the Communications entry show the exact date.
  • It isn't entered in Briostack. Honor it at booking by entering the lower price.

Inspection / Consultation and One Time Service customers

Same policy: what they paid for that visit becomes a credit toward the plan. Example: they paid $247, so the $447 Critter Control fix is $200.

Cancellations

If a customer cancels one plan, the other goes back to its regular price

  • The lower monthly fee only holds while they have both plans.
  • Example: they cancel Critter Control, so Signature goes from $44 back to $59 a month.
  • Change it by hand in Briostack as part of the cancellation procedure.
